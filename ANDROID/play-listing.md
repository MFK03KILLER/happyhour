**Short description** (≤80)
```

**Full description** (≤4000)
```
Happy Hour is a membership that turns your everyday going-out into everyday saving.

One membership. A new deal every day. At real places near you.

WHAT YOU GET
• A fresh member deal every day at partner restaurants, cafes, bars and venues
• Buy-one-get-one offers, percentage discounts, free items and exclusive perks
• Surprise bags — great food from local kitchens at a fraction of the price
• A map of partner venues near you, with directions in one tap

HOW IT WORKS
1. Browse the deals near you
2. Tap Claim — your coupon appears instantly with a QR code
3. Show the code at the counter. The staff scans it. Done.

No printing. No coupon codes to type. No awkward conversations.

WHY MEMBERS LOVE IT
• Save more than the membership costs in your first couple of outings
• Discover places in your neighbourhood you did not know existed
• Everything is in your wallet — nothing expires unnoticed
• Cancel any time, right from the app

WHERE IT WORKS
Happy Hour partners with independent restaurants, cafes, bakeries, steakhouses, bars and venues across the San Francisco Bay Area, and we are adding new places every week.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Deals are redeemable during each venue's happy-hour window (typically weekdays 2–5 PM) and exclude public holidays
• One coupon claim per day per member
• Your membership unlocks discounts you redeem in person, at the venue

Questions? support@happyhourz.org
Privacy policy: https://happyhourz.org/privacy
Delete your account: https://happyhourz.org/delete-account
```

```

**Short description** (≤80)
```
Scan member coupons, track redemptions and manage your venue's offers.
```

**Full description** (≤4000)
```
Happy Hour Merchant is the staff app for venues that partner with Happy Hour.

FOR YOUR COUNTER
• Scan a member's QR code in under a second and confirm the discount
• Instant, unambiguous confirmation — no paper coupons, no guesswork
• Works on any phone or tablet your team already has

FOR YOUR MANAGERS
• See every redemption as it happens, with time and staff member
• Daily and weekly stats: how many members visited, what they redeemed
• Manage which offers are live and during which hours
• Set holiday closures so offers pause automatically

FOR OWNERS WITH SEVERAL LOCATIONS
• Manage every location from one account
• Compare performance between venues
• Add team members with the right level of access

This app is for Happy Hour partner venues. Your account is created when you join the programme.

Want to become a partner? partners@happyhourz.org
Privacy policy: https://happyhourz.org/privacy
```
